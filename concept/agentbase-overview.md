## Why use Dashboards & CRM

You want to build a "system" for your business — a place to manage customers, track inventory, follow projects, or schedule content. In the past, you'd have to figure out which tables you need, design the fields, fill in the data row by row, and configure dashboards yourself. **Dashboards & CRM lets you say in one sentence what you want to manage, and hands the rest to AI**: it builds the database, fills in the data, and generates a first view you can look at right away — a working system in minutes. You don't need to know anything about databases; it all happens through conversation. And the system is **persistent and reusable** — add more data anytime, view it different ways, schedule automatic updates, and share it with teammates.

Core value: **From "I want to manage X" to a system you can actually use is just one sentence away — building tables, filling in data, and creating views are all done by AI. You just say what you want and make the calls.**

## What it is

Dashboards & CRM (internal codename AgentBase) is a product that **has AI build and run your business systems over the long term**. You describe what you want to manage, and AI:

- Builds a **database** with real data (multiple tables that can relate to each other)
- **Presents** the data in multiple ways — tables, boards, galleries, calendars, forms, plus interactive dashboards
- Pulls data in from your **email, files, other apps (like HubSpot, Salesforce, Dropbox, Microsoft 365), and databases**
- Automatically updates and sends reports on a **schedule** you set (daily / weekly / monthly / custom), and can also **watch a metric you care about and push an alert to your phone** when it goes off track
- **Shares** with teammates and supports role-based collaboration; a single dashboard can also be shared on its own as a read-only link, even to people without an account

The whole process requires no query writing or schema design — you just talk in everyday language.

## What I can build

Anything that's "a bunch of items you want to manage in a structured way" works:

- **Customer relationships (CRM)**: customers, contacts, deals, follow-up notes
- **Inventory & orders**: products, stock levels, incoming, outgoing
- **Content calendar**: topics, status, publishing platforms, schedule
- **Recruiting pipeline**: candidates, roles, interview stages, evaluations
- **Personal lists**: reading notes, budgets, travel plans, goal tracking

Not sure where to start? Just type a one-sentence description of what you want to manage in the home page input box, or pick the closest match from the template library to get going.

## About credits

Dashboards & CRM **has no separate per-action pricing** — it's **billed by conversation usage**. Each message you send, and each time AI builds tables, fills in data, imports, generates views, or replies, that **entire round** is settled once based on usage. Specifically:

- **Browsing/picking templates, creating/renaming/deleting workspaces, viewing already-generated boards, and exporting table data** — these don't call AI and are **free**
- **Having AI build tables, import data, generate views, query, and reply** — billed by the usage of that conversation round
- **Scheduled automation workflows** — each background run is likewise billed by usage (your credit balance is checked before each run)
- **Background checks for metric monitors** — they just verify the data without going through AI, and cost no extra credits

> Actual consumption is shown in the interface, and you'll be prompted if you run low on credits.

## FAQ

**I know nothing about databases/programming — can I use this?**
Yes. The whole process is a conversation — you say in everyday language what you want to manage and how you want to view it, and AI handles all the table building and data processing. What you see are intuitive things like tables and boards; you never touch any technical details.

**How is this different from just opening an AI Sheets table myself?**
Spreadsheets are good for a single table and some calculations. Dashboards & CRM is a **multi-table relational system** — for example, in a CRM, "Customers" and "Deals" are two tables that relate to each other, plus you can build boards, calendars, and dashboards, schedule automatic updates, and share with role-based access. To run a category of business long-term, use this.

**Will the system I build stay around?**
Yes. Each system is a persistent workspace — come back anytime to keep adding data, switch views, and share. You can see all your workspaces on the home page.

**Can I build several systems at once?**
Focus on one system per conversation. To switch to a different system or create another, just go back to the home page or start a new conversation. See [Create a system](https://page.gensparksite.com/manual/buddy-guides/v1/en/agentbase-create-a-system.md) for details.

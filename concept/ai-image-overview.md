## Why use AI Image

When you need an image—a graphic, a poster, a product shot, a concept draft—you usually have to know the software, know how to draw, or hunt for assets over and over. AI Image collapses that whole process into a single sentence: describe what you want, and get a ready-to-use, high-quality image in seconds. Not happy with it? Just say one more thing to tweak it, or repaint, erase, expand, cut out, or sharpen right where you are—no tool-switching needed.

Core value: **from a single sentence, or one uploaded image, get a ready-to-use finished image—and keep refining it in place until you're happy.**

## What you can do

- **Text-to-image**: describe the scene you want in natural language and generate it directly
- **Edit uploaded images**: upload a local image and go straight into the editing canvas to refine it (repaint / erase / expand / cut out / sharpen)
- **Multiple models to choose from**: several built-in image models—pick one manually, or let the system choose automatically (available options follow what's shown in the UI)
- **Tune image parameters**: aspect ratio, size, clarity/quality, style, camera (focal length/aperture), and more, adjusted as needed (adjustable options vary by the selected model)
- **Edit in place**: on a generated result, directly repaint, erase objects, expand the canvas, cut out the background, boost clarity, or generate another version
- **Find inspiration**: the landing page has a sample gallery grouped by model, where you can reference others' prompts and results
- **Revisit history**: Recents keeps the images you generated most recently

## How it differs from generating images elsewhere

- **Conversational, continuous edits**: it doesn't end once generation is done—you can adjust sentence by sentence, or open the in-place editing tools to refine.
- **Generation + editing in one**: cutting out, expanding, erasing, upscaling—operations that usually need separate tools—are all done in one place.
- **It helps you write prompts**: turn on Auto Prompt to have the AI automatically expand your brief description into a more complete generation instruction.

## About Credits

- **Generating images consumes credits**—every generation or edit (repaint/erase/expand/cut out/sharpen all count as a re-generation) deducts credits.
- **Paid models pop up a "credit consumption confirmation dialog" before generating** so you confirm before continuing; free models generate directly with no confirmation.
- **Some models can be used without spending credits** (which models are free, and how high-resolution/high-quality tiers are assigned, follow what's shown in the UI).
- A **failed generation refunds** the credits for that attempt; nothing is finally charged before the generation process completes.
- Pricing, membership tiers, and benefit comparisons follow the **live pricing page** ([genspark.ai pricing page](https://www.genspark.ai)).

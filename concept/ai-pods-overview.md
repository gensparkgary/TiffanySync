## Why use AI Pods

Want to turn an article, a video, a research paper, or "today's tech headlines" into a podcast you can listen to right away? Doing it yourself means choosing a topic, writing a script, finding someone to voice it, recording, editing, and adding music — that's hours of work, plus you need the equipment and the voice. AI Pods lets you **just give one sentence**: what to cover, what language, how many hosts, what style — and the AI handles all the research, scripting, voiceover, music, and cover art. A few minutes later you get a podcast that's **ready to listen to and ready to share**.

Core value: **One sentence in, one finished podcast out — research, script, host voiceover, and audio are all automated, and in minutes you get a podcast you can listen to, share, and export as MP3.**

## What you can do

- **Generate from any source**: a topic, an article/web page, a YouTube video, or an uploaded PDF/document — all can become a podcast
- **Customize in one sentence**: state the language, number of hosts, and style in your prompt, and the AI follows your request
- **Multilingual**: supports generation in multiple languages (per what the UI shows)
- **Host conversations**: two hosts in conversation by default, or you can request a single narrator
- **Automatic cover art**: a podcast cover image is generated by default and can be regenerated
- **Built-in player**: listen right after generation, adjust playback speed, skip forward/back, and view the Script
- **Video version (optional)**: when you need visuals, you can generate a video version with slide visuals
- **Publish / Share / Export**: publish to the AI Pods community homepage, share a project link, or export as a downloadable MP3

## How AI Pods differs from making audio elsewhere

| What you want to do | Which to use |
|--------|--------|
| Generate **a whole podcast** from one sentence (auto research + scripting + two-host voiceover + music + cover) | **AI Pods** |
| Turn already-written text into **a single voice clip / narration** (TTS, voice cloning) | AI Audio (text to speech) |
| Add a **podcast-style narration video** to an existing video/slides | You can also generate a video version in AI Pods, or generate a video in AI Slides |

Not sure → if you want "a publishable podcast with hosts in conversation," use **AI Pods**; if you just want "a single narration audio clip," use AI Audio.

## About credits

**Generating a podcast consumes credits** — it includes content research, scriptwriting, host voiceover (TTS), and cover image generation, making it a complete AI creation; generating the **video version** consumes extra. After generation, **Publish (to the community), Share (link), Export (MP3), and Save / favorite are all free**.

> Actual consumption is per what the UI shows. Generation usually takes a few minutes (the UI indicates 5–15 minutes).

## FAQ

**How is this different from making a podcast myself with recording equipment?**
Doing it yourself means choosing a topic, writing a script, voicing it, recording, editing, and adding music. AI Pods does all of that from one sentence, producing a finished podcast with host conversation and cover art in minutes — ready to publish or export as MP3.

**Can I edit it after it's generated?**
You can regenerate the cover image. Other content like the script can't currently be edited in place — to change the content, regenerate a new pod with an updated prompt. See [Languages and creation options](https://page.gensparksite.com/manual/buddy-guides/v1/en/ai-pods-voices-and-options.md).

**Can I export it as a file?**
Yes, you can export as MP3: click Export to save to AI Drive and then download, or open the audio and use Download in the top-right of the new tab. See [Publish and export](https://page.gensparksite.com/manual/buddy-guides/v1/en/ai-pods-publish-and-export.md).

## What is AI Drive

AI Drive is Genspark's built-in cloud drive — a single place to store and manage all the files you create and use across Genspark.

What sets it apart from an ordinary cloud drive is this: **every Genspark AI product is connected to your cloud drive**. Presentations created by Slides, documents written in Docs, materials downloaded locally by AI, files produced by AI coding — they all automatically land in your cloud drive. And the other way around, files you upload to your cloud drive can be handed directly to Super Agent, AI Slides, and more for processing. No more exporting and importing files back and forth between products.

## Core Value

- **One place to manage all your Genspark files**: No need to find a separate home for each product's output — presentations, documents, sheets, images, videos, and downloaded materials all live in the same cloud drive, ready whenever you come back for them.
- **Files connect directly to AI**: Any file in your cloud drive can be handed off to AI with a single click (turn it into a presentation, generate a video, run deep research…), and the files AI produces go straight back to your cloud drive — no more downloading and re-uploading.
- **Built for sharing and collaboration**: Any file or folder can be shared by inviting people by email (view-only or editable) or via a link; shared items appear in the recipient's "Shared with me" tab, and folders support collaborative editing by multiple people.

## Storage Space

The cloud drive offers different storage quotas by plan — the higher the plan, the more space — **refer to the membership page / cloud drive interface for the exact capacity**.

The top of the cloud drive shows your used space and total capacity. When you run out of space, uploads are blocked and you're prompted to upgrade. Downgrading your membership tier reduces your available space accordingly.

## Cost

**File management in the cloud drive is free** — uploading, downloading, organizing, compressing, sharing, and deleting/restoring consume no credits; storage is a membership benefit.

Only when you **hand a file off to AI for processing** (for example, having Super Agent analyze it, turn it into a presentation, or generate a video) are you charged according to that AI product's rules — those costs belong to the downstream product, not to the cloud drive itself.

## FAQ

**Q: Are AI Drive and Google Drive / OneDrive the same thing?**
No. AI Drive is Genspark's own cloud drive, connected to Genspark's AI products. You can also import files from Google Drive / OneDrive to use in Genspark, but those are separate, external cloud drives.

**Q: Can free users use it?**
Yes. The cloud drive is open to all users, and the free tier includes a certain amount of storage (refer to the interface for the exact capacity). File management operations like uploading, organizing, and sharing are free.

**Q: Do files generated by Slides / Docs automatically save to the cloud drive?**
Yes. Output from Genspark's products automatically lands in your cloud drive, where you can find everything in one place.

**Q: Do recipients need a Genspark account to view files I've shared?**
Link access requires the visitor to sign in to a Genspark account; people you invite by email who don't have an account yet get access after they register and sign in for the first time. See the [sharing guide](https://page.gensparksite.com/manual/buddy-guides/v1/en/aidrive-share.md) for details.

## Why use AI Meeting Notes

The most exhausting part of meetings is **participating and taking notes at the same time**—take notes and you can't keep up with the discussion, follow the discussion and your notes are incomplete, and afterwards you still have to spend time turning it all into shareable minutes. AI Meeting Notes lets you **just focus on the meeting**: record it or let a Bot join automatically, and the moment it ends you get notes complete with a full transcript, summary, key points, and action items—ready to send to attendees, ask the AI follow-up questions about, or archive.

Core value: **Focus on the meeting, leave the notes to AI—from recording to ready-to-send meeting minutes, all done automatically.**

## What it can do

- **Three ways to capture a meeting**: record on the spot, upload existing audio/video, or let a Meeting Bot automatically join and record an online meeting
- **Automatic transcription**: turn audio into a transcript, auto-detect the spoken language, and capture every line even in multilingual meetings
- **AI-generated notes**: summary, key points, decisions, and action items, produced automatically the moment a meeting ends
- **Speaker identification**: automatically distinguish different speakers, and rename each one to their real name
- **Q&A and editing**: ask follow-up questions about meeting content, or have the AI edit the notes for you directly
- **Search**: search titles/summaries across all meetings, and search keywords within a transcript
- **Share and export**: email to attendees in one click, generate a share link, export Word/text/subtitle files, save as PDF, or save to Notion

Works across Web, iOS/Android, and Apple Watch (double-tap your watch to start recording), with recordings syncing automatically.

## How to choose among the three capture methods

| Method | Best for | How to start |
|------|---------|---------|
| **On-the-spot recording** | In-person meetings, face-to-face discussions, quick notes | Go to the home page and tap **New note** to start recording |
| **Upload audio/video** | Audio/video files you've already recorded | Click the arrow on the right of **New note** and choose **Import file**, then pick a file in the Import Audio dialog |
| **Meeting Bot** | Online meetings (Zoom/Meet/Teams, etc.) without manual effort | Connect your calendar and let the Bot join automatically, Join manually, or paste a meeting link directly (Add meeting URL) |

The arrow menu on the right of **New note** actually has **four** items: **Start recording**, **Capture with Speakly** (subtitled "Voice dictation app — with smarter Meeting Notes built in", tagged "FREE FOR PLUS & PRO" — an entry to the Speakly voice-dictation app), **Add meeting URL**, and **Import file**.

Not sure → choose **on-the-spot recording** for in-person meetings, and **Meeting Bot** for online meetings (fully automatic once you connect your calendar).

## About credits

Recording, upload transcription, and Meeting Bot recording consume credits based on **duration**, and every user gets a certain amount of **free time**; generating summaries, identifying speakers, and so on are all included in transcription with no extra charge. Asking follow-up questions about meeting content (Chat) uses standard conversation consumption. Editing notes, renaming speakers, generating share links, Export direct downloads, Save to Notion, and copying are all **free**. **Save as PDF consumes credits**—it's an AI generation (laying out the notes into a PDF saved to AI-Drive), not an ordinary local export.

> Actual free time and credit consumption are as shown in the interface.

## FAQ

**How is this different from just using a voice recorder/phone recording?**
A voice recorder only gives you audio—you still have to listen and organize it yourself. AI Meeting Notes gives you a transcript + summary + action items + speaker identification directly, and you can also ask follow-up questions and share it.

**My meeting is online—do I still have to tap record manually?**
No. After connecting your calendar, enable Auto-join for the Meeting Bot, and the Bot will automatically join scheduled events that have a meeting link and record them, generating notes automatically when they end. See [Meeting Bot and calendar](https://page.gensparksite.com/manual/buddy-guides/v1/en/meeting-notes-bot-and-calendar.md).

**Which platforms are supported?**
Web, iOS/Android App, and Apple Watch (watchOS 10.0 and above). On the watch, double-tap to record, and it syncs automatically to your phone and the Web.

**Can I download the original recording file?**
No. For privacy protection, only the transcript and notes text are kept—the original audio is not saved.

# Genspark Membership — How Credits Work

> For Buddy Agent internal use.
> type: concept | feature: membership | keywords: Credit, quota, balance, consumption, recharge, validity, add-on, usage

## What Are Credits

Credits are the universal currency on the Genspark platform, used to measure your usage of AI features. Different AI operations consume different amounts of Credits—for example, generating a video costs more than a single AI chat.

> The monthly Credit allotment for each membership tier, along with the allotment and price of add-on packs, all follow the [live pricing page](https://www.genspark.ai/helpcenter/credits-guide) (these change over time, so no numbers are listed here).

## Where Credits Come From

- **Free users**: A small amount of Credits is granted automatically every day, enough to try out basic features.
- **Monthly membership recharge**: Once you subscribe, the system automatically recharges your tier's Credits **every month**—no manual action needed. Annual plans are not granted all at once for the whole year; instead, they're credited **monthly just like monthly plans** (split into 12 deliveries across the year).
- **Additional add-ons**: If you run low mid-month, you can either upgrade to a higher tier or buy a standalone Credit pack (which doesn't change your tier). If it's just a temporary shortfall, **add-on packs are usually more cost-effective than upgrading**.

## Credit Validity

- **Monthly membership recharge** Credits: Use them up **before your next recharge**; any unused Credits expire and reset to zero.
- **Free daily** Credits: Valid for that day only.
- **Additional add-on** Credits: Have a **longer** validity than monthly recharge Credits.

> The system automatically **uses your soonest-to-expire Credits first**, helping you make the most of your balance.
>
> You can check the **specific expiration date** of each batch of Credits on the [Credit Usage page](https://www.genspark.ai/credit-usage).

## Which Operations Consume Credits

The vast majority of AI features consume Credits (AI Chat, image/video/audio generation, AI Slides, AI Sheets, AI Docs, Genspark Design, and more). Consumption is affected by these factors:

- **Input length**: Longer content and larger uploaded files cost more
- **Conversation depth**: The longer the context across multiple turns, the more it costs
- **Media type**: Video > Image > Audio > Text
- **Model / mode**: More powerful models or modes cost more

> The interface will show how much each operation costs as you use it. Plus / Pro don't consume Credits in certain scenarios (such as AI Chat and AI Image)—**refer to the pricing page for specifics**.
>
> **If an AI generation fails or is interrupted, no Credits are deducted.**

## How to Check Your Credit Balance and Usage

- **Current balance**: Shown directly in the **account / avatar menu in the bottom-left corner**.
- **Detailed usage**: Go to the [Credit Usage page](https://www.genspark.ai/credit-usage) (also accessible from the account menu), where you can see everything in one place:
  - Remaining / used Credits
  - **Consumption breakdown** by **each AI feature / product**
  - **Historical consumption details** (how much each operation cost)
  - The start and end times of your **subscription cycle** and **current monthly recharge cycle** (the cycle's end is when the next recharge / renewal happens)
  - The **expiration date** of each batch of Credits

## How Credits Are Calculated When Upgrading or Downgrading

- **Upgrade**: There are two cases, distinguished by **whether you also switch billing cycles**—
  - **Upgrading within the same billing cycle** (e.g., Plus monthly → Pro monthly, or moving up a tier within the same cycle): Your existing Credits are **kept**, and the **full monthly allotment difference is granted immediately** (new tier's monthly allotment − old tier's monthly allotment), **with no proration by remaining days**. The granted Credits expire at the end of the current cycle. For subsequent months: on a **monthly plan**, only this one batch is granted for the current month, and the full new-tier amount is granted when you renew next month; on an **annual plan**, since Credits are already delivered monthly, after upgrading the **remaining months will also automatically be granted at the new tier's allotment**.
  - **Upgrading with a billing cycle switch** (e.g., Plus monthly → Pro **annual**): Your **unused Credits from the old plan are voided**, and the **full Credit amount under the new plan is granted immediately** (counting from the moment of upgrade), with no "difference top-up."
  - Note: Proration by remaining days applies to **the amount you need to pay that day**, not the number of Credits granted. For the exact amount due that day and the Credits granted, **refer to the upgrade confirmation dialog**.
- **Downgrade**: The current cycle's Credits remain usable until the end of the cycle; **once the next cycle begins, they reset to the new tier's allotment**, and old Credits do not carry over.

## What to Do When You Run Out of Credits

The interface will show a prompt, and you can:

1. **Upgrade your tier**: Move to a tier with a higher Credit allotment
2. **Buy a Credit add-on pack**: Purchase extra Credits separately (without changing your tier); usually more cost-effective than upgrading for temporary shortfalls
3. **Wait for your next recharge**: Members are automatically recharged at the start of the next cycle

> Both upgrading and adding on are done on the plans page: [click here to open the plans page directly](https://www.genspark.ai/me?open_pricing=help_center).

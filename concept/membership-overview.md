## Three Account Types

Genspark has three account types:

- **Free**: Get a small amount of Credits each day to try out the basics; advanced capabilities like all models, larger storage, and commercial use are restricted.
- **Plus**: Built for everyday / light-to-moderate use—more monthly Credits, access to stronger models, larger storage, and commercial usage rights.
- **Pro**: Built for heavy / professional use—a higher Credit allowance than Plus, larger storage, plus some exclusive high-resolution image models.

> **For exact prices, the Credit allowance for each tier, and a detailed Plus/Pro benefits comparison, always refer to the live pricing page** (it gets updated, so no numbers are listed here): [Membership & Pricing](https://www.genspark.ai/helpcenter/membership-plans)

## Monthly vs. Annual

Every tier supports both monthly and annual billing. Annual billing is charged once per year and **works out cheaper on a per-month basis**. You can switch between the two prices anytime at the top of the pricing page.

## How to Choose

Don't get hung up on tier names—pick based on your actual usage:

- **Everyday chat, occasional image generation** → Plus (the entry tier is plenty)
- **Frequent image / video / slide generation** → a higher Plus tier or entry-level Pro
- **Heavy video, professional images, team-level output** → Pro
- **Need exclusive high-resolution models** → Pro

> Not sure which tier to pick? The [pricing page](https://www.genspark.ai/helpcenter/membership-plans) gives the clearest view of each tier's Credit allowance and price—just choose based on your rough monthly usage.

## Usage Rules

To ensure service quality for all users, AI features have reasonable rate limits (sending a large number of requests in a short time may trigger temporary throttling).

The following are prohibited: bulk automated scraping, account sharing or reselling, and large-scale data extraction—violations may result in account suspension.

> Membership subscriptions are tied to an individual account and **cannot be transferred between accounts**.

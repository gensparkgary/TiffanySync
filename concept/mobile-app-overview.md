## What is the Genspark App

The Genspark App is Genspark's official mobile application (App Store name: **Genspark AI Workspace**; on Android, search **Genspark** in Google Play). It puts the AI workspace you use on the web right in your pocket — ask questions, build slides, work on spreadsheets and docs, generate images and videos, take meeting notes, all wherever you go.

**Same account, same credits, same projects as the web version**: tasks you kick off on your phone can be picked up on your computer, and projects you left half-done on your computer are ready to open on your phone anytime.

## How the App differs from the web version

| Aspect | Details |
|---|---|
| **Native experience for core products** | Chat, AI Slides, AI Sheets, AI Docs, AI Image and AI Video, AI Notes, and more have native interfaces designed for the phone screen in the App — not a web wrapper |
| **Some products open as web pages** | A few products (e.g., Genspark Code, Deep Research) open inside the App as embedded web pages, with the same functionality as the web version and an input bar at the bottom so you can keep the conversation going |
| **Phone-only capabilities** | Push notifications when tasks finish, sharing content from any app straight into a question, opening certain Genspark share links directly in the App, voice input and Realtime Voice conversations, and connecting the SecondBrain Note recording device |

One practical note: **complex, fine-grained editing (like deep layout reworks) is limited by the phone screen** — the App itself will suggest that such tasks are better on a desktop browser. The phone is great for starting tasks, tracking progress, and light edits; go back to your computer for heavy editing.

## FAQ

**Can I use it offline?**
No — the App requires an internet connection. On a weak network it will prompt you to switch networks; just retry once your connection is back.

**Can I be logged into multiple accounts at once?**
No — the App is signed into one Genspark account at a time; to switch accounts, log out and log back in.

**Is there a dark mode toggle?**
The App follows your phone's system light/dark setting automatically — there's no separate toggle.

**What is the GenMail at the bottom?**
GenMail is Genspark's standalone email app; tapping it takes you to the GenMail App (and prompts you to install it if you haven't yet).

**Can I see what I made on my phone from my computer?**
Yes. All projects, files, and notes sync to the cloud under the same account, so you can continue on either device.

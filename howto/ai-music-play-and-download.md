## Why this page

Once your music is generated, what you really want to do is **listen, keep, and use** it: confirm how it sounds, download it as a file, pick one from the track cards across multiple models, or — if you're not happy — regenerate with a follow-up prompt. This page covers everything you can do after generation.

## Prerequisites

- You've already generated at least one piece of music with AI Music (see [Creating Music](https://page.gensparksite.com/manual/buddy-guides/v1/en/ai-music-create.md))
- Listening, downloading, and comparing track cards across different models is **free**; regenerating with a follow-up prompt consumes credits again (deducted when generation reaches its final state)

## Steps

### 1. Listen (built-in player)

Once generation completes, the audio card on the right comes with its own player: hit play/pause and drag the progress bar to listen. With the default MoA, you'll get **multiple track cards** (one per model) side by side, so you can listen to and compare each one.

![Generation result: built-in player + one track card per model + Assets panel](https://gensparkpublicblob.blob.core.windows.net/user-upload-image/v1/pr_upload/36111/5d350a27.png)

### 2. Download

Click the download / **View Full** entry on the card, and the audio file opens in a new tab where you can save it locally.

> What you download is an audio file, which you can use for videos, presentations, personal listening, and more.

### 3. Keep it in the Assets panel

Generated music is **automatically collected into the Assets panel on the right** (and synced to AI Drive), so there's no need to manually "add" it — **there's no separate "Add to library" button**. Each asset card has a **multi-select checkbox** in its top-right corner, so you can select multiple tracks for batch operations.

### 4. Compare versions across models

When you use the default **Auto-select Models** (MoA) or ask for multiple models in your request, the result is **one independent track card per model** (as many cards as models used; the exact models are whatever the interface shows), displayed **side by side** in the Assets panel on the right — just play each one to compare. **This is not a V1 / V2 version-switching tab.**

> Listening to and comparing the multiple track cards you've already generated doesn't consume credits — they're all results from the same generation run.

### 5. Regenerate / get another version

AI Music is conversational, with **no dedicated "Regenerate" button**: if you're not happy with the result, send a follow-up request in the input box at the bottom (e.g. "Give me another version, a bit more upbeat" / "Regenerate with a different model"), and the AI will generate it again. **Regenerating consumes credits again** (it has to run a fresh generation, deducted when it reaches its final state).

## About cover art, publishing, and sharing

AI Music focuses on **generating + listening + downloading**: the result is an audio file, with **no auto-generated cover art** and **no "publish to community feed" or music-specific Share button** (the top bar only has the project-level Share common to all agents, defaulting to a Restricted link). The landing page also has no "For You" recommendation feed. When you want to use the music elsewhere, just **download the file** — the result is also automatically kept in the Assets panel / AI Drive.

## FAQ

**What format is the download?**
You get an audio file, which you can save locally to play or use in videos/presentations (the exact format is whatever the downloaded file is).

**Does comparing the track cards cost credits?**
No. The multiple cards are all results from the same generation run, so listening and comparing is free. Only regenerating with a follow-up prompt consumes credits again.

**Can I save music to use later?**
Yes. Generated music is automatically kept in the Assets panel on the right / AI Drive, and you can also download it as a file directly.

**Is there cover art? Can I publish or share it?**
AI Music's result is an audio file, with no auto-generated cover, no community publish button, and no music-specific Share — to share it, just download the file and send it (the project-level Share in the top bar is the Restricted link common to all agents).

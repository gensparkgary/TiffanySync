> User loop: Generate a podcast → publish to the community / share a link / export and download MP3
> Help Center：https://www.genspark.ai/helpcenter/ai-pods

## Why use publishing & exporting

- **Get it out there**: Publish to the AI Pods community homepage with one click so more people can discover your podcast.
- **Share privately**: Generate a shareable project link to send to specific people.
- **Grab the file**: Export as MP3 to save locally or use elsewhere.
- All of these are **free** — billing happens when the podcast is generated, so publishing/sharing/exporting won't cost any credits.

## Prerequisites

- A podcast already generated (see [Creating AI Pods](https://page.gensparksite.com/manual/buddy-guides/v1/en/ai-pods-create.md)) with audio available
- Entry point: the action bar above the generated result (Publish / Share / Export)

## Steps

### 1. Publish to the community (Publish)

Click **Publish** and confirm — your podcast will then appear on Genspark's **AI Pods community homepage**, where others can discover and listen to it.
![Publish to the community: click the Publish button at the top of the right panel](https://gensparkpublicblob.blob.core.windows.net/user-upload-image/v1/pr_upload/36052/d979a7f1.png)

After publishing, the button shows **Published**. Note: publishing **jumps you to the AI Pods community feed**. To unpublish, you'll need to **return to this podcast** and click **Published** again to remove it from the community.

### 2. Share a link (Share)

Click **Share** in the top-right corner to open the **project share dialog**:

- **General Access**: defaults to **Restricted** (only invited people can access); you can change it to a more open permission level
- **Copy link**: copy the project link to send to others
- **Invite by email**: enter an email in the dialog to grant access to specific people

Once you've set the permissions, copy the link and send it out — the recipient will access it according to the permissions you set.

> Publish makes it public on the community homepage; Share sends a project link to specific people (based on permissions). The two can be used independently.

### 3. Export MP3 (Export)

There are two ways to get the MP3 file:

**Option A — Export to AI Drive, then download**: Click **Export**, and the podcast is first saved to your AI Drive, from which you can download it to your local machine.
![Export: click Export Audio to save the podcast to AI Drive, then download the MP3](https://gensparkpublicblob.blob.core.windows.net/user-upload-image/v1/pr_upload/36052/6a762a4b.png)

**Option B — Download in a new tab**: Click the podcast audio file to open it in a new tab, then click **Download** in the top-right corner.

> If you generated a **video version**, the Export option will show **Export Video**, and it exports the video file instead.

### 4. (Optional) Save / Bookmark / Add to Hub

You can **Save** the podcast or **Bookmark** it to find it again later. The **⋮** menu in the result area also has **Add to Hub**, which lets you add this podcast to your Hub workspace.

## FAQ

**What's the difference between Publish and Share?**
Publish makes your podcast **public** on the AI Pods community homepage, where anyone can discover it. Share generates a **project link** that you send to specific people based on the permissions you set.

**How do I take it down after publishing?**
Click the button again (it shows **Published** at this point) to unpublish.

**What format does it export to?**
Audio exports as MP3. If you generated a video version, it exports the video file.

**Do publishing/sharing/exporting cost credits?**
No. The podcast is billed when it's generated, so publishing, sharing, exporting, and bookmarking afterward are all free.

## Why Use Design for Presentations and Documents

- **Free-form design**: Not limited by traditional PPT templates — every page can be fully customized
- **Three multi-page formats**: Slide deck (presentation), Flipbook (page-turning book), Paged Book (long document) — covering different needs
- **Fullscreen presentation**: Built-in Present mode + Speaker Notes
- **Multiple export formats**: Supports PPTX (PowerPoint) and PDF

## Comparing the Three Multi-Page Formats

Before you start, understand the differences between the three formats:

| | **Slide deck** | **Flipbook** | **Paged Book** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Presentation (for an audience) | Visual page-turning book (self-paced browsing) | Long-form text document (auto-layout) |
| Page turning | Slide transitions | Manual page turn | Continuous scroll / paged |
| Content style | Key points + charts + data | Each page is a standalone visual piece | Continuous text, auto-paginated |
| Best for | Fundraising pitches, quarterly reviews, training decks | Picture books, photo collections, recipes, lookbooks, product catalogs | Textbooks, white papers, academic papers, technical manuals |
| Fullscreen presentation | ✅ Present + Speaker Notes | ✅ Page-turn preview | ✅ Reading view |
| Export formats | PPTX / PDF | PDF | PDF |

**Recommendations**:
- Need to present on stage → **Slide deck**
- Want people to flip through, each page a standalone visual → **Flipbook**
- Need to write long text with automatic pagination → **Paged Book**
- Not sure → **Slide deck** (the most versatile)

## Slide deck (Presentation)

### 1. Create a Slide deck

Click the **"Slide deck"** button, or just describe what you want:

- "Create a 12-slide Series A pitch deck"
- "Design a quarterly business review presentation"
- "Make a training course deck about data analytics"

The AI will generate a complete multi-page presentation.

### 2. Present and Export

- **Fullscreen presentation**: The **Present** button in the Canvas top bar. Slide deck supports Speaker Notes (per-page notes that only you can see while presenting). Present works for all design types — see [Export and Share](https://page.gensparksite.com/manual/buddy-guides/v1/en/design-export-and-share.md) for details.
- **Export as PPTX**: Say "Export as PPTX" in chat to automatically download a .pptx that opens in PowerPoint / Google Slides / Keynote.
- **Export as PDF**: Click **"More"** → **"Save as PDF"**.

## Flipbook (Page-Turning Book)

### 1. Create a Flipbook

Describe what you want in chat:

- "Create a picture book about a little fox's adventure"
- "Design a recipe collection flipbook with 10 dishes"
- "Make a product lookbook for our spring collection"

The AI will generate a design in flipbook format, where each page is a standalone visual piece.

### 2. Page-Turn Preview

Every page in a Flipbook can be turned manually, letting you browse it like a real book. It's perfect for visual works meant to be enjoyed page by page.

### 3. Export

Flipbook supports exporting to PDF format.

## Paged Book (Long Document)

### 1. Create a Paged Book

Describe what you want in chat:

- "Create a 20-page user manual for our app"
- "Design a white paper about AI in healthcare"
- "Make a textbook chapter about machine learning basics"

The AI will generate a long-form document and automatically lay out the continuous text into clean, multi-page layouts.

### 2. Automatic Pagination

The core feature of Paged Book is **automatic pagination**:

- Long text content is automatically paginated based on page size
- Supports headers, footers, and page numbers
- Charts and images automatically flow into the page layout
- Table of contents is generated automatically

### 3. Export

Paged Book supports exporting to PDF — ideal for printing or distribution.

## Keep Editing Within Your Presentation

No matter which format you use, you can keep making changes in chat:

- "Change slide 3 to include a chart instead of a bullet list"
- "Add two more pages about market analysis"
- "Make the fonts larger throughout the document"
- "Change the color theme to match our brand"

You can also use [Tweaks](https://page.gensparksite.com/manual/buddy-guides/v1/en/design-canvas-tools.md) to quickly adjust colors, font sizes, and other parameters, or use [Draw](https://page.gensparksite.com/manual/buddy-guides/v1/en/design-canvas-tools.md) to sketch the changes you want directly on a slide.

## FAQ

### What's the difference between Slide deck and AI Slides?

Both can create presentations. **AI Slides** is a standalone PPT tool (Professional/Creative modes), while **Slide deck** is a design type within Genspark Design. Slide deck runs inside Design's all-in-one Canvas, so you can use all of Design's tools (Tweaks, Comment, Build it, and more).

### Can I convert a Flipbook into a Slide deck?

There's no direct conversion. But you can describe "Recreate this content as a slide deck" in chat, and the AI will regenerate it.

### What's the maximum number of pages for a Paged Book?

There's no hard limit. The AI paginates automatically based on the amount of content. For very long documents, we recommend generating them chapter by chapter.

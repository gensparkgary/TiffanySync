## Why Use Design for Visual Content

- **AI image generation**: The images in posters are generated by AI in real time — no need to hunt for stock assets
- **Layer system**: Each image in a poster is an independent layer that can be regenerated individually
- **One-click PDF export**: Generate print-ready PDFs and send them straight to print

## Designing a Poster

### 1. Select the Poster Type

Click the **"Poster"** button above the input box, or just describe your poster needs:

- "Design a music festival poster with psychedelic style"
- "Create a minimalist product launch flyer"
- "Design a conference event poster for a tech summit"

### 2. Review the AI-Generated Poster

The AI generates a complete poster design, including AI-generated images, headlines, and text layout. Posters use a **layer system** — each visual element (image, text region) is an independent layer.

### 3. Working with Layers

Poster designs come with a built-in **poster toolbar** that lets you operate on individual layers:

- **Select a layer**: Click an image or region in the poster
- **Regenerate**: Have the AI generate a new image for the selected layer
- **Adjust position and size**: Drag to move or scale a layer

> This is a poster-only feature — other design types require changes through chat descriptions, while posters let you manipulate layers directly.

### 4. Keep Fine-Tuning

Continue describing changes in chat:

- "Make the title larger and change the color to gold"
- "Replace the background image with a cityscape"
- "Add a QR code in the bottom right corner"

## Fine-Tuning and Exporting

- **Make Tweakable**: Have the AI generate Tweaks controls for your design, so you can quickly adjust parameters like titles, dates, and colors by dragging. See [Canvas Editing Tools](https://page.gensparksite.com/manual/buddy-guides/v1/en/design-canvas-tools.md) for details.
- **Export PDF**: Print-ready PDFs. See [Exporting and Sharing](https://page.gensparksite.com/manual/buddy-guides/v1/en/design-export-and-share.md) for details.
- **Export Image / PSD**: Poster projects have an **Export** menu in the Canvas top bar to export a PNG image, or a layered Photoshop (.psd) file — each poster layer becomes a Photoshop layer with text kept editable, so you can keep polishing in Photoshop. See [Exporting and Sharing](https://page.gensparksite.com/manual/buddy-guides/v1/en/design-export-and-share.md) for details.

## FAQ

### What sizes do posters support?

The AI automatically picks the right dimensions based on your description. You can also specify explicitly, for example "Design an A3 poster" or "Create a 1080x1080 Instagram post".

### Can I batch-generate multiple similar posters?

Yes. Generate one, then use Make Tweakable, and quickly adjust the title, date, and other content through Tweaks — each adjustment takes effect in real time. You can also say in chat, "Create 5 variations of this poster with different color schemes".

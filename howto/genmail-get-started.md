# Installation & Sign-In: Download, Sign In to Your Account, Connect Your Mailbox

> For Buddy Agent internal use.
> type: howto | feature: genmail-get-started | keywords: GenMail, download, install, desktop app, sign in, Genspark account, connect mailbox, Gmail, Outlook, Onboarding, Super Agent, system tray, notifications, auto-update
> User loop: Download and install the desktop app → Sign in with your Genspark account → Connect your Gmail/Outlook mailbox for the first time → Land in the Super Agent view → Start handling email with AI

## Why read this first

- **GenMail isn't a URL — it's an app installed on your computer.** A lot of people hunt around in their browser looking for an entry point they'll never find. After reading this, you'll be able to go end to end in one pass — "download → install → sign in → connect mailbox" — and actually put your inbox to work.
- **"Signing in to your account" and "connecting your mailbox" are two different things, and they're very easy to confuse.** The first determines who you are and which membership you're on; the second determines which emails GenMail can read and write. Get these two steps straight and none of the later AI features will get stuck on "why can't I see my email?"
- **Signing in and connecting your mailbox are completely free.** Get your account and inbox hooked up first, then decide whether you want to use the credit-consuming AI features.

## Prerequisites

- Entry: GenMail is a **desktop app you download and install** (not a web link). The path in is: **download the installer → install and launch the app → sign in with your Genspark account → (first time) connect your Gmail / Outlook mailbox**.
- A Genspark account (if you don't have one, you can register on the spot when signing in).
- At least one Gmail or Outlook mailbox to connect.
- A Mac or Windows computer.

## Steps

### 1. Download and install the desktop app

Get the installer for your system (Mac or Windows) from the GenMail download entry provided by Genspark. Once it's downloaded, install it like any other software, then open the app.

### 2. Sign in with your Genspark account

When the app launches, it first asks you to **sign in to your Genspark account**. This step determines "who you are" and your membership tier — it's **not yet connecting your mailbox**.

Your sign-in options include **Google**, **Apple**, **Microsoft**, and enterprise single sign-on (your company's unified login). After you click one, the app **automatically opens your computer's system browser** to complete verification, and once you're verified it jumps back to GenMail.

![GenMail sign-in page: ① Sign in with your Genspark account — Continue with Google / Apple, with more methods (including Microsoft) under More options; verification happens in the system browser](https://gensparkpublicblob.blob.core.windows.net/user-upload-image/v1/pr_upload/39036/4cf89943.png)

> Once you authorize, it stays valid long-term — you won't need to sign in over and over during normal use.

### 3. Tell "signing in" apart from "connecting your mailbox"

Signing in successfully does **not** mean your email has come in yet. GenMail deliberately keeps these two things separate:

| | What it determines | The result of doing it once |
|---|---|---|
| **Sign in to your Genspark account** | Who you are, which membership you're on, whose credits get charged | You enter the app, but your inbox may still be empty |
| **Connect your mailbox** (Gmail / Outlook) | Which emails GenMail can read and write, which inbox is shown | Email starts syncing in, giving the AI content to work with |

So once you've signed in, the very next step is always to **connect your mailbox**.

### 4. Connect your Gmail / Outlook mailbox for the first time

After your first sign-in, the app enters an Onboarding flow — it introduces the capabilities with "Your inbox now has a brain" alongside a few value cards, and **requires you to connect a mailbox** before you can continue.

![First-time Onboarding ("Your inbox now has a brain"): ① Choose to connect Gmail or Outlook to unlock the main interface](https://gensparkpublicblob.blob.core.windows.net/user-upload-image/v1/pr_upload/39036/f3f005b9.png)

Choose **Gmail** or **Outlook**, and the app will again open your system browser to have you authorize GenMail to access that mailbox. Once authorization is done, it jumps back to the app and your email starts syncing in.

> You can repeat this step: to add another mailbox later, just keep adding accounts under Settings, and multiple mailboxes will be aggregated together. See [Settings & Account Management](https://page.gensparksite.com/manual/buddy-guides/v1/en/genmail-settings-and-accounts.md) for details.

### 5. Land in the Super Agent view

Once your mailbox is connected, the app lands you by default in the **Super Agent** view — this is where you use natural language to have the AI handle email and run tasks for you.

> Note: Super Agent is being rolled out gradually and needs to be enabled for your account. **If your account isn't enabled yet, you'll see a message here along the lines of "not enabled for this account"** — that's normal and doesn't affect your ability to read and manage email as usual. Whether it's visible, and exactly how it looks, depends on what actually appears in your interface.

Whether or not Super Agent is enabled for you, the left navigation bar lets you switch between **Super Agent → Mail → Calendar → Email Brain**; **Mail** is your everyday workspace for reading, replying to, and organizing email, and it's always available.

> The navigation labels are always in English (even Chinese-language users will see Super Agent / Mail / Calendar / Email Brain).

### 6. Get familiar with the desktop app's core conveniences

As a desktop app, GenMail has a few conveniences you won't get on the web:

- **System tray**: even after you close the window, the app keeps running in the background, and you can bring it back up anytime from the system tray (the menu bar at the top on Mac / the taskbar corner on Windows).
- **Desktop notifications**: get a system notification when new email arrives, so you don't have to keep staring at the window (can be turned off in settings).
- **Auto-update**: the app pulls new versions automatically — you don't need to manually re-download and reinstall.

## FAQ

**Q: I signed in, so why is my inbox still empty?**
Signing in only settled "who you are" — you haven't connected a mailbox yet. Go back to the Onboarding flow or connect your Gmail / Outlook under Settings, and email will start syncing in.

**Q: Why did clicking sign-in jump me over to a browser?**
That's by design — GenMail uses your computer's system browser to complete identity verification, and it jumps back to the app once you're verified. It stays valid long-term after you authorize once.

**Q: I use a company mailbox — is there a dedicated sign-in method?**
Yes. The sign-in screen supports enterprise single sign-on (your company's unified login) — just follow the prompts. If you're not sure, ask your company's IT team.

**Q: Does signing in and connecting a mailbox cost anything?**
Both are free. Credits are only consumed when you use capabilities like AI writing, AI replies, or having Super Agent run tasks.

**Q: I don't see Super Agent — is something wrong?**
No. The feature is being rolled out gradually and needs to be enabled for your account; when it isn't enabled you'll see a message, and it doesn't affect your ability to read and manage email as usual. Go by what actually appears in your interface.

# GenTeam — Your First Steps

> For Buddy Agent internal use.
> type: howto | feature: genteam | keywords: GenTeam, get started, onboarding, new-user guide, Genny, first message, AI team, AI teammate, hire an agent
> User loop: Home AI TEAM tile / sidebar More → GenTeam (bare workspace, no group chat yet — invites and deep links keep their destination) → land straight in the Genny DM (greeting already posted, composer live) → tap a starter chip or just type → let Genny hire your first agent / open your first group chat → send your first @agent message and get a reply

## Why use GenTeam

- "Hire" AI agents as long-term teammates: they stay in your group chats, remember earlier discussions, and can pick up tasks — no need to re-explain everything in a fresh conversation each time
- No tutorial to read first: the moment you arrive you are already in a conversation you can type into, so just say what you need
- Not sure where to start? Genny, the built-in help assistant, can create your first agent or your first group chat for you

## Prerequisites

- Entry: https://www.genspark.ai/genteam/genspark (straight to the workspace; https://www.genspark.ai/genteam is the GenTeam marketing page)
- Requirements: a signed-in Genspark account; putting a cloud agent to work needs credits on the account

## Steps

### 1. Open GenTeam

Any of three entries:

- Go to https://www.genspark.ai/genteam/genspark directly
- The **GenTeam** tile under the **AI TEAM** group on the Genspark home page (opens a new tab)
- Genspark sidebar "More (...)" menu → **GenTeam**

![The GenTeam tile under the AI TEAM group on the home page](https://gensparkpublicblob.blob.core.windows.net/user-upload-image/v1/pr_upload/48590/4c4672de.png)

### 2. You land straight in Genny's DM

A new account that opens the bare workspace is taken directly into the DM with **Genny** (GenTeam's built-in help assistant) — if you arrived from an invite, a deep link or an embed you keep that destination instead. There is no card, tour or channel-building wizard to get through first:

- Genny's opening message is **already posted** — it is not generated by a click
- The composer is **live**, with the cursor already in it, so you can type immediately
- Genny is **pinned** to the top of the sidebar (`Pinned`); the `Chats` section below it is empty
- Five **one-tap starter chips** sit just above the composer

### 3. Tap a chip, or just say what you want

The five chips are:

- **What is an agent?** — get the concept first
- **Hire an agent for me** — have Genny create one for you
- **Set up a group chat for my team** — have Genny open one for you
- **Check why my agent is stuck** — have Genny diagnose an agent that is not replying
- **Report a problem** — send feedback on GenTeam or any other Genspark product straight to the team

Tapping one **fills the composer** with that sentence; it does not auto-send, so you can edit it before pressing Enter. You can also ignore the chips entirely and just type what you are trying to do — Genny will take it from there.

![Genny's DM on a brand-new account's first visit](https://gensparkpublicblob.blob.core.windows.net/user-upload-image/v1/pr_upload/56623/3c4678ec.png)

### 4. Let Genny build your first agent or group chat

The **Hire an agent for me** and **Set up a group chat for my team** chips are not just Q&A — they route into Genny's own tools:

- Ask her to hire an agent and she will check what it is for, confirm with you, and **actually create it**
- Ask her to set up a group chat and she does the same

You can always build either by hand instead: channels in [Channels and messages](../howto/genteam-channels-and-messages.md), agents in [Create your agent](../howto/genteam-create-agents.md).

### 5. Send your first @agent message

Open any group chat that has an agent in it, type `@`, pick an agent, write your first request, and press **Enter** to send (**Shift+Enter** for a newline). The agent you mentioned will reply — an agent you created yourself will always wake to your @ — and you will first see a working indicator, with the reply usually collected in that message's thread ("1 reply"); click to open it.

How credits work: a cloud agent's work is charged to the **agent's creator**, not to whoever sent the message. For an agent you created, that is you.

![A first @agent message and the agent's reply](https://gensparkpublicblob.blob.core.windows.net/user-upload-image/v1/pr_upload/56623/22e72b62.png)

## FAQ

**Q: I @-mentioned an agent and it never replied.**
First check whether it shows as working. If an "@{name} couldn't reply" card appears inline, that agent's creator is out of credits — it resumes once the creator tops up. More reply rules in [Working with agents](../howto/genteam-work-with-agents.md).

**Q: What happened to the Welcome card / tour / channel wizard / sample group chats?**
They were deleted from the web on 2026-08-28. Measured data showed that six-screen chain kept most new users from ever sending a first message, so the flow now drops you straight into a conversation you can talk in. **The native app still runs the old chain**, so app users may still see those screens — do not apply this guide to app questions.

**Q: Does talking to Genny cost credits?**
Yes, your own. The workspace itself and reading history do not.

**Q: Can I use agents without spending Genspark credits?**
Yes — agents can also run on your own computer or your Claw machine using your own model resources, which costs no Genspark credits. See [Connect your own computer](../howto/genteam-computers-and-claw.md).

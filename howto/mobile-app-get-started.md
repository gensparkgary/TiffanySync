# Genspark App — Quick Start

> For Buddy Agent internal use.
> type: howto | feature: mobile-app | keywords: download, install, login, App Store, Google Play, home, bottom navigation, task list, get started
> User loop: Install via store/QR code → Log in (or browse anonymously first) → Learn the home screen, bottom navigation, task drawer → Find any product entry and start your first task

## Why install the Genspark App

- **Your AI workspace, on the go**: Everything you can do on desktop — research, slides, docs, images, videos — starts with a single sentence on your phone too, so you can kick off tasks on your commute.
- **Your phone knows when a task is done**: Hand a long task to the AI, lock your screen and walk away — you'll get a push notification when it's finished so you can come back for the results.
- **Seamless handoff on one account**: Start on your phone, refine on desktop. Projects and credits stay in sync throughout.

## Prerequisites

- Where to get it: On iPhone, search **Genspark AI Workspace** in the App Store; on Android, search **Genspark** in Google Play. Or open `genspark.ai/download` on your computer and scan the QR code on the Genspark App card.
- A Genspark account (you can also browse first without logging in and sign up on the spot when prompted)

## Steps

### 1. Download and install

Search and install from the app store, or scan the QR code on the `genspark.ai/download` page to go straight to the store page.

### 2. Log in (or browse first)

On first launch you can browse the home screen directly — logging in isn't required. When you're ready to start, tap Log in in the top right:

- **Continue with Google**: One-tap login with your Google account
- **Continue with Apple**: iPhone only
- **Sign up (Free) / Log in**: Sign up or log in with email — this opens a web page and returns to the App automatically when done

![Login panel: Google / Sign up (Free) / Log in](https://gensparkpublicblob.blob.core.windows.net/user-upload-image/v1/buddy-guides/mobile-app/gs-login.png)

### 3. Get to know the home screen

After logging in, the home screen from top to bottom:

- **Input box** ("Ask anything, create anything"): The main entry point for asking questions — see [Asking and Voice](https://page.gensparksite.com/manual/buddy-guides/v1/en/mobile-app-chat-and-voice.md)
- **Suggestions**: A horizontally scrolling row of task inspiration cards — tap one to start with that scenario
- **Product grid**: Tiles for common products like AI Slides, AI Sheets, AI Docs, AI Chat (as shown in the UI); the last tile, **All Agents**, opens the full product list
- Top right: **Upgrade** (upgrade your membership) or **Add Credits** (top up credits) — see [Subscription and Settings](https://page.gensparksite.com/manual/buddy-guides/v1/en/mobile-app-subscribe-and-settings.md)

![Home screen: input box, product grid and All Agents](https://gensparkpublicblob.blob.core.windows.net/user-upload-image/v1/buddy-guides/mobile-app/gs-home.png)

### 4. Bottom navigation

| Tab | Content |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Home screen (ask questions + product entries) |
| **AI Notes** | Meeting notes: record, import, view notes (see the [AI Meeting Notes guide](https://page.gensparksite.com/manual/buddy-guides/v1/en/meeting-notes-record-and-import.md) for usage) |
| **GenMail** | Jumps to the standalone GenMail email App; guides you to install it if not already installed |
| **More (⋯)** | Opens three entries: **Skills / AI Drive / Hub** |

![More panel: Skills / AI Drive / Hub](https://gensparkpublicblob.blob.core.windows.net/user-upload-image/v1/buddy-guides/mobile-app/gs-more.png)

### 5. Task list and switching between pages

- Tap the **top-left button** (or swipe right from the left edge of the screen) to open the **task list drawer**: your project history sorted by time, a search box at the top, and your account card at the bottom (tap it to open settings). Tap any past project to pick up the conversation right where you left off.
- When you have multiple projects open, use the **page switcher overlay** to switch between them (like tabs in a mobile browser).

![Task list drawer](https://gensparkpublicblob.blob.core.windows.net/user-upload-image/v1/buddy-guides/mobile-app/gs-drawer.png)

## FAQ

**What can I do without logging in?**
You can browse the home screen and product list. Asking questions, AI Notes, Skills, AI Drive, Hub and more require login — a login panel appears when you tap them.

**Are the features the same on iPhone and Android?**
Core features are identical. Differences: iPhone offers an extra Apple account login option; in-app purchases go through the App Store / Google Play respectively.

**I can't find the product I want in the home grid?**
Tap **All Agents** at the end of the grid to see all products. Some products (like Genspark Code and Design) open as built-in web pages, but none of the functionality is missing.

**Is AI Notes the same thing as AI Meeting Notes on the web?**
Yes — same product, same data. Notes you record on your phone show up on desktop too.

# Genspark App — Push Notifications & Sharing

> For Buddy Agent internal use.
> type: howto | feature: mobile-app | keywords: push, notifications, share, opening links, save to album, reminders
> User loop: Enable push → get a phone reminder when a long task finishes; share content from any app → it instantly becomes source material for a Genspark question; receive a Genspark link → open it directly inside the App

## Why use these features

- **No need to babysit the screen**: hand a long task off to the AI and go do something else — a push notification will let you know when it's done.
- **See it, ask about it**: an article in your browser or a file received in a chat app can be shared to Genspark and turned into a question in one step, saving you the copy-paste shuffle.
- **Links land instantly**: when someone shares a Genspark project link, tap it and view it right inside the App — no need to open a browser and log in first.

## Prerequisites

- Genspark App installed and logged in (see [Quick Start](https://page.gensparksite.com/manual/buddy-guides/v1/en/mobile-app-get-started.md))

## Steps

### 1. Enable task-completion push notifications

The first time it's needed, the App shows an explainer layer (letting you know push is used for task-completion reminders); confirm, then allow the system permission prompt that follows. After that, you'll get a push when an Agent task finishes or a background task makes progress; when the App is in the foreground, it shows a banner at the top.

If you missed the chance to enable it, just turn on Genspark's notification permission in your phone's system settings.

### 2. Share content into the App

In any app, tap the system **Share** and choose Genspark:

- Share **text or a link** → opens a new conversation with the content pre-filled in the input box (not sent automatically); add your notes and submit
- Share **files (including multiple)** → they become question attachments in a new conversation

![Choosing Genspark in the system share panel](https://gensparkpublicblob.blob.core.windows.net/user-upload-image/v1/buddy-guides/mobile-app/ns-share.png)

### 3. Open Genspark links directly in the App

Some Genspark share links can open directly inside the App (such as Super Agent task share links and AI Drive shared links); other Genspark links open in the browser. For those that open in the App: if you have access you can view them, read-only projects show a read-only view, and when you need edit access you can tap **Request edit access** to ask the owner.

### 4. Save images and videos to your album

Images and videos generated by the AI in a conversation can be saved directly to your phone's photo album (the first time, it will request album permission).

## FAQ

**Not receiving push notifications?**
Check in order: whether Genspark's notification permission is enabled in your phone's system settings; whether your phone's battery-saver mode / background restrictions are blocking Genspark; and whether your network is working.

**Nothing happens after sharing?**
Make sure the share target you picked is Genspark (not another AI app); large files take time to upload, so wait for the attachment to appear before submitting.

**Does sharing count against credits?**
The share action itself is free; once you submit it as a question, it's billed like a normal question.

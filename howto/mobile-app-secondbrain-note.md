# Genspark App — SecondBrain Note Device

> For Buddy Agent internal use.
> type: howto | feature: mobile-app | keywords: SecondBrain Note, voice recorder, recording device, hardware, pairing, Bluetooth, transcription, SBN
> User loop: Home / AI Notes entry → pair device via Bluetooth → record on the go + add markers → auto-transcribe on sync → get a usable note in AI Notes

## Why use SecondBrain Note

- **Capture without pulling out your phone**: Meetings, ideas, thoughts on the go — press the device once to start recording while your phone stays in your bag.
- **Recordings become notes automatically**: Once a recording syncs, AI transcribes and organizes it, so you get a searchable, editable, shareable note right in AI Notes.
- **Mark highlights on the spot**: Drop a marker during recording so you can jump straight to key moments on review.

## Prerequisites

- A SecondBrain Note recording device
- A logged-in Genspark App with phone Bluetooth turned on
- Auto-transcription consumes credits (the App explains this clearly during pairing, and you can turn off auto-transcription)

## Steps

### 1. Find the device entry

If you haven't paired before, the home screen shows a **SecondBrain Note Device — Your AI Note taker** card; you can also get in from the device slot under the bottom **AI Notes** tab.

![SecondBrain Note device entry on the home screen](https://gensparkpublicblob.blob.core.windows.net/user-upload-image/v1/buddy-guides/mobile-app/sbn-entry.png)

### 2. Pair the device

Follow the App's prompts to power on the device (long-press the front button). Once the App's Bluetooth scan finds the device, complete the pairing. During pairing, a **transcription mode notice** pops up: auto-transcription (hassle-free, consumes credits) or manual transcription — read the notice before choosing, and you can change it later in settings.

### 3. Record and mark

The device records on its own; during a recording you can mark the current moment in the App: **Note** (text annotation), **Photo** (attach a photo), or **Flag** (highlight flag). When reviewing a note, use the markers to jump to those points.

### 4. Transfer and transcribe

Once the device is connected to your phone, recordings sync automatically; when there are a lot of files, use **Fast Transfer** (a direct device-to-phone transfer for faster speeds). After sync completes, notes are produced according to your chosen transcription mode and can be viewed in **AI Notes**. Anything recorded offline won't be lost — it uploads once you reconnect the device online.

### 5. Manage the device

On the device details page you can: check connection and battery status (you're reminded on low battery), switch between multiple devices, update device firmware, and erase device data.

## FAQ

**Is transcription charged?**
Auto-transcription consumes credits (explained during pairing); if you'd rather not be charged automatically, turn off auto-transcription and manually transcribe individual recordings when needed.

**Will offline recordings be lost?**
No. Recordings are stored on the device and sync automatically the next time it connects to your phone.

**Where do I view notes, and how do I edit and share them?**
All in **AI Notes** (bottom tab). For the full workflow on editing, exporting, and sharing, see the [AI Meeting Notes guide](https://page.gensparksite.com/manual/buddy-guides/v1/en/meeting-notes-review-and-edit.md).

**Can I use AI Notes without this device?**
Yes. AI Notes supports recording directly on your phone and importing audio; the device is a "no phone needed" enhancement. See the [AI Meeting Notes guide](https://page.gensparksite.com/manual/buddy-guides/v1/en/meeting-notes-record-and-import.md).

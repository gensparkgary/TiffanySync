# Genspark App — Subscriptions & Settings

> For Buddy Agent internal use.
> type: howto | feature: mobile-app | keywords: subscription, upgrade, membership, credits, credits, in-app purchase, restore purchases, cancel subscription, refund, settings, language, delete account
> User loop: Upgrade entry point → complete a subscription or buy a credits pack (store payment) → manage/cancel/restore as needed; tune language, privacy, and account on the Settings page

## Why subscribe inside the App

- **Handle it right on your phone**: Upgrade your membership or top up credits without switching to a computer. Payment goes through the App Store / Google Play, confirmed with Touch ID / Face ID.
- **Membership works everywhere**: The membership and credits you buy in-app are exactly the same as on the web, and take effect instantly across all platforms.

## Prerequisites

- Signed in to the Genspark App
- Payment completed through your phone's app store (App Store on iPhone, Google Play on Android)

## Steps

### 1. Upgrade your subscription

Entry point: **Upgrade** in the top-right of the home page, or the plan card on the Settings page. Once the payment panel opens:

1. Select the **Upgrade Plan** tab
2. Choose a plan tier, and toggle **Monthly / Annually** (the annual option has a discount badge)
3. Confirm and complete the store payment

### 2. Buy a credits pack

When you're low on credits but don't want to change tiers: switch to the **Buy Credits Pack** tab in the payment panel and pick a pack to buy. Your balance is always visible on the account card in Settings.

### 3. Manage, cancel, and restore

- **Manage/cancel subscription**: Settings page **Manage Subscription** → jumps to the store's subscription management page (in-app subscriptions are managed by the store and can't be changed on the web)
- **Restore Purchases** (iPhone): If your subscription doesn't show up after switching phones or reinstalling, tap **Restore Purchases** on the Settings page
- **Refunds and billing**: Refunds and invoices for in-app purchases go through the respective app store; see the [Membership Subscription Guide](https://page.gensparksite.com/manual/buddy-guides/v1/en/membership-subscribe.md) for the process

### 4. Settings page tour

Tap your account card at the bottom of the task drawer to enter **Settings**:

| Section | Contents |
|---|---|
| Account card | Avatar and nickname (editable), plan, and credits balance |
| Features | AI Notes settings (calendar connection, meeting bot), Custom Dictionary, Connectors, Personalization (help the AI understand you better: how to address you, occupation, personality preferences) |
| Content | Management of your shares, favorites, and other content (items shown per your account's content) |
| Preferences | **Select Language** (interface language, can follow system), **Data Retention** (AI data retention), network check |
| Bottom | Rate us, Terms & Privacy, feedback, sign out |

![Settings page](https://gensparkpublicblob.blob.core.windows.net/user-upload-image/v1/buddy-guides/mobile-app/ss-settings.png)

### 5. Account management

From the account card on the Settings page, open your personal info: change your name, view login sessions (you can kick out other devices), and **Delete Genspark Account**. Before deleting, note: **cancel any in-app subscription at the store first**. Deleting your account won't automatically stop store charges — the App will remind you of this during the deletion flow.

## FAQ

**What's the difference between subscribing in the App and on the web?**
The membership benefits are exactly the same. The difference is the payment and management channel: in-app purchases are collected and managed by the store (both cancellation and refunds happen at the store); web subscriptions are managed on the web. You only need to subscribe in one place per account.

**Do credits work across phone and computer?**
Yes — it's the same balance for the same account.

**Will my subscription still be there after switching phones?**
Yes. Just sign in to the same Genspark account. On iPhone, if it doesn't show up, tap Restore Purchases.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
For in-app purchases: Settings → Manage Subscription to cancel at the store. For web subscriptions: go to the account page on the web. If you're not sure where you subscribed, see the [Membership Subscription Guide](https://page.gensparksite.com/manual/buddy-guides/v1/en/membership-subscribe.md).

# Second Brain — Telegram Quick notes

> For Buddy Agent internal use.
> type: howto | feature: secondbrain | keywords: Second Brain, Quick note, Quick notes, quick notes, quick notes, Telegram, bot, connect, scan QR code, daily notes, daily notes, Disconnect
> User journey: Home "Quick note" pill → scan QR to connect the Telegram bot → send a message to the bot → message lands in a daily note → view the card stream on the page / jump to the editor to edit → Disconnect when needed (content is kept)

## Why use it

- Ideas, to-dos, and passing thoughts — just send a message to the Telegram bot on your phone and it's saved into Second Brain; organize everything later when you're back at your computer
- Second Brain is built for desktop browsers, and Telegram Quick notes is its official lightweight entry point on mobile
- Everything you jot down is automatically filed by date and becomes part of your memory, so it can be referenced later when you ask questions

## Prerequisites

- Entry point: https://www.genspark.ai/second-brain/home (built for desktop browsers)
- Requirements: signed in to your Genspark account; Telegram installed on your phone (or computer)
- Connecting, sending messages, and saving notes are all free and don't consume credits

## Steps

### 1. Open the Quick notes page

On the Second Brain Home page, click the **Quick note** pill below the Ask input box to open the Quick notes page.

![The Quick note pill below the Ask input box on the Home page](https://gensparkpublicblob.blob.core.windows.net/user-upload-image/v1/pr_upload/48745/08638b25.png)

![Quick notes page (not yet connected): connection guide, 3-step walkthrough, and Connect button](https://gensparkpublicblob.blob.core.windows.net/user-upload-image/v1/pr_upload/48745/5c55e6fe.png)

### 2. Scan the QR code to connect the Telegram bot

Click **Connect** on the page — only then does the real, scannable QR code appear in the dialog (the QR code in the walkthrough illustration is just a placeholder and can't be scanned). Scan it with your phone's camera to open the official bot **@GensparkSecondbrainBot**, and starting a conversation completes the connection. At the bottom of the dialog there's also an **Or open in Telegram** link — if Telegram is installed on this device, you can click it to connect directly without scanning.

![The dialog after clicking Connect: a real scannable QR code, @GensparkSecondbrainBot, and the Or open in Telegram link](https://gensparkpublicblob.blob.core.windows.net/user-upload-image/v1/pr_upload/48745/0aa1c1f8.png)

### 3. Send a message to the bot, and it lands in a daily note automatically

Once connected, any message you send to the bot is automatically recorded into Second Brain:

- Messages are organized by date into **daily notes**, stored in the **Telegram** folder within your documents
- No commands or formatting needed — just send whatever comes to mind

### 4. View and edit on the page

- The Quick notes page displays what you've jotted down as a **reverse-chronological card stream**, with the newest at the top
- Click a card to jump to the editor and edit that day's note just like a normal document

### 5. Disconnect when needed (content is kept)

If you no longer want to use it, click **Disconnect** on the Quick notes page to remove the connection. All the notes you've already saved are kept and won't be lost when you disconnect.

## FAQ

**Q: Do Quick notes cost credits?**
No. Connecting, sending messages, saving notes, and editing are all free; credits are only consumed by usage when you later ask questions about that content (Ask/Chat) — see [Ask and Chat](https://page.gensparksite.com/manual/buddy-guides/v1/en/secondbrain-ask-and-chat.md).

**Q: Does Second Brain have a mobile app?**
No. Second Brain is built for desktop browsers, and its official lightweight entry point on mobile is Telegram Quick notes — jot things down on your phone, then organize and ask questions back on the desktop.

**Q: Are my notes still there after I Disconnect?**
Yes. Disconnecting only removes the Telegram connection; all captured content stays in the Telegram folder, and you can reconnect and continue recording anytime.

**Q: Where are the messages I send stored, and can I edit them?**
They're filed by date in the Telegram folder within your documents, and like any normal document they can be edited in the editor — see [Notes and file management](https://page.gensparksite.com/manual/buddy-guides/v1/en/secondbrain-notes-and-files.md).

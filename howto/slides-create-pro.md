# AI Slides — Creating with Professional Mode

> For Buddy Agent internal use.
> type: howto | feature: ai-slides | keywords: Professional, create, PPT, generate
> User loop: /ai_slides → choose Professional → pick aspect ratio → pick Tier → enter prompt → generate → see the full deck → edit/export

## Why use Professional Mode

- **Delivers business decks you can actually use**: reports, proposals, training, analysis—describe your topic in one line and get a fully structured, finished deck
- **Information that's instantly clear**: titles, key points, charts, and lists are laid out automatically, so your audience can follow your logic effortlessly
- **Painless revisions**: edit text and images directly, with minor tweaks costing no credits—so you can adjust calmly right before a meeting
- **Get started even when you don't know where to begin**: categorized preset options give you a professional framework, saving you from staring at a blank page

## Prerequisites

- Login required
- Entry point: `/ai_slides` or the AI Slides icon in the sidebar

## Steps

### 1. Open the creation page

Open `/ai_slides`. On your first visit, an onboarding welcome dialog appears (titled "AI Slides", subtitled "More Powerful and Efficient than Ever!"). Click **"Try it now"** or **"Maybe later"** to close it.

![Onboarding welcome dialog ("AI Slides" — Try it now / Maybe later)](https://gensparkpublicblob.blob.core.windows.net/user-upload-image/v1/pr_upload/51105/d5df162b.png)

![AI Slides creation page](https://gensparkpublicblob.blob.core.windows.net/user-upload-image/v1/pr_upload/51105/cbfea77c.png)

### 2. Select Professional mode

The page defaults to Professional mode (the **"Professional"** tab is highlighted). If you're currently in Creative mode, click **"Professional"** to switch.

The difference between the two modes:
- **Professional**: structured business decks, template-driven
- **Creative**: full-canvas AI visual design

### 3. Choose the aspect ratio

Click the aspect ratio selector (default **Auto**) and pick from the dropdown:

| Option | Best for |
|------|---------|
| **Auto** | AI picks automatically (default) |
| **16:9** | Meeting presentations, projection |
| **4:3** | Traditional projectors |
| **1:1** | Square social media |
| **3:4** | Portrait posters |
| **9:16** | Portrait Stories |
| **21:9** | Ultrawide screens |

![Aspect ratio dropdown](https://gensparkpublicblob.blob.core.windows.net/user-upload-image/v1/pr_upload/36120/648db159.png)

### 4. Choose a Tier (optional)

The Tier selector lets you switch between **Ultra** and **Standard**. **Ultra** is selected by default (labeled **"Best choice"**) and puts quality first; **Standard** (with an **"All-new"** badge) is faster and more economical. For the exact credit cost of each tier, go by what the tier selector shows in real time.

See [slides-tiers explained](https://page.gensparksite.com/manual/buddy-guides/v1/en/slides-tiers.md) for details.

![Tier selector (Standard "All-new" / Ultra "Best choice", default Ultra)](https://gensparkpublicblob.blob.core.windows.net/user-upload-image/v1/pr_upload/51105/bf84d001.png)

### 5. Turn on Guide Mode (optional)

For high-stakes scenarios (pitch decks, board reports), we recommend turning on the **Guide Mode** toggle. Once enabled, the Agent first runs several rounds of in-depth consultation to fully understand your needs before generating.

See [Guide Mode explained](https://page.gensparksite.com/manual/buddy-guides/v1/en/slides-guide-mode.md) for details.

### 6. Choose a Skill (optional)

Browse preset Skills across 15 categories in the Skill Gallery below:

| Category | Description |
|------|------|
| Coursework | Class reports, academic presentations |
| Corporate Strategy | Business strategy |
| Training | Training materials |
| B2B Sales | Sales proposals |
| Academic | Academic research |
| Marketing | Marketing plans |
| Data & KPI | Data analysis, KPI reports |
| Fundraising | Fundraising pitches |
| More... | Public Policy, Product Mgmt, Consulting, Career, AI Literacy, Life, Design Craft |

The Skill Gallery has two tabs at the top, **Skills / Recents**. Under **Skills** there are further **All / My Skills / Featured** subfilters, with cards arranged by category below. Click **"Add & Use"** on a card to use that Skill; you can also click a card to open its detail dialog (with preview samples) to review before adding.

You can use multiple Skills at once—each one you select is stacked into the input area as a chip, and you can keep adding more. See [Skill Gallery explained](https://page.gensparksite.com/manual/buddy-guides/v1/en/slides-skill-gallery.md) for details.

![Browsing Skill Gallery by category](https://gensparkpublicblob.blob.core.windows.net/user-upload-image/v1/pr_upload/36120/41b9ed9e.png)

### 7. Enter a prompt and generate

Describe your presentation topic in the input box. Next to the input box are **voice input** (microphone icon) and **attachment upload** ("+" icon) buttons. Click send. For example:
- "Q3 quarterly business review for the marketing team"
- "AI industry trends 2026 presentation for investors"

The Agent starts generating, which usually takes a few minutes to complete.

![Entering and sending a prompt](https://gensparkpublicblob.blob.core.windows.net/user-upload-image/v1/pr_upload/36120/bf3f3a97.png)

### 8. Review the generated result

Once generation is complete, you enter the **Slides workspace**:

- The conversation area is on the left (with a summary of the deck and data sources); the canvas area is on the right
- An **outline thumbnail rail** sits on the left of the canvas with the current slide center stage — you can watch AI build the deck page by page and click a thumbnail to jump
- The toolbar above the slide offers the **Select / Draw / Edit** editing modes and the **Verify content** button
- The top of the workspace has **Present**, **Export** (PDF/PPTX/Google Slides), and **History** buttons plus the **⋮ menu** (Presentation View / Make a Copy / Save as Skill)

You can keep entering edit instructions in the conversation, or use the toolbar to edit. See [Editing and refining](https://page.gensparksite.com/manual/buddy-guides/v1/en/slides-edit-and-refine.md) and [Exporting and sharing](https://page.gensparksite.com/manual/buddy-guides/v1/en/slides-export-and-share.md) for details.

## FAQ

- **How do I choose between Standard and Ultra?** Ultra is the default tier (labeled "Best choice")—quality first; Standard (badged "All-new") is faster and more economical. Use Standard for everyday drafts and Ultra for high-stakes scenarios; the tier selector shows each tier's live credit cost.
- **How many Skills can I use at once?** There's no fixed limit—each Skill you select is stacked into the input area as a chip, and you can keep adding as needed.
- **Does "Add & Use" save the Skill to My Skills?** No. Clicking "Add & Use" only applies that Skill to the current creation.
